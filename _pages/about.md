# 💼 Projects & Experience

- **Image Refinement with Binary-Quantized Generative Models**  
  Proposed a two-stage framework based on [Infinity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04431) to correct artifacts in images generated by [Stable Diffusion v1.4](https://huggingface.co/CompVis/stable-diffusion-v1-4). The method performs deterministic image inversion via LoRA-based optimization and refines localized defects using a masked self-attention module. Comprehensive quantitative and qualitative evaluations demonstrate improvements over state-of-the-art baselines.

- **Anomaly Detection System on the [MURA](https://stanfordmlgroup.github.io/competitions/mura/) Dataset**  
  Developed a DenseNet-based anomaly detection framework leveraging final-layer feature representations for classification and HiresCAM for post-hoc localization and interpretability. Conducted systematic benchmarking against ResNet and VGGNet variants, with DenseNet achieving superior performance. The system was deployed as both Android and web applications to enhance accessibility for end users.

- **Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competitions**  
  Presented a project at the 2022 “Construction Bank Cup” Zhejiang Provincial Internet+ College Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition and represented Zimbabwe as an Ambassador at the 2024 China International College Students’ Innovation Competition.
